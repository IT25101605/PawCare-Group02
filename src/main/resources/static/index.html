How do I get started with PawCare?

Create an account, select Pet Owner, and add your pet's profile. From your dashboard, you can book vet appointments, arrange grooming, and keep track of your pet's care.

Can I manage more than one pet?

Of course. Add each pet to your account and keep their details and appointments organized in their own profile.

Where can I find my appointments and orders?

Sign in and open your dashboard. Your appointments, grooming bookings, and store orders each have their own easy-to-find section.

How can I talk to the PawCare team?

Visit our contact page for our phone number, email, and location. We're happy to help you find the right care.
